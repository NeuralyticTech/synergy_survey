Staff Feedback Survey

Your chance to share how you're feeling

✓ Your answers are private
Only your manager will read them. Nothing here puts your role at risk. This is a check-in, not a test.

How to Take the Survey

  1. Visit the survey link below on your phone or computer
  2. Pick your company (Portal Technology or Synergy)
  3. Enter your work email to verify it's really you
  4. Check your email for a 6-digit verification code
  5. Enter the code to unlock the survey
  6. Answer the questions (~15 minutes, be honest)
  7. Submit — you're done
synergy-staff-survey.vercel.app

What to Expect

The survey covers:

  • Your role and how you're getting on
  • Tools and support you need
  • How you feel about the culture
  • What's working well and what could improve
  • Your goals and where you want to go

There are no right or wrong answers. We want to hear how you're actually feeling.

Questions?

One optional answer: if you'd rather discuss something in person instead of writing it down, just say so in your answer. You can raise it one-to-one with your manager instead.

Privacy: Your name stays on the survey so your manager can follow up with you personally. All responses are encrypted and locked until your manager logs in. No one else sees them.

Questions? Ask your manager or team lead.

Thank you for taking the time. Your feedback helps us improve.
