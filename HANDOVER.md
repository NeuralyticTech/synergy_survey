# Staff Feedback Survey — Quick Start

Your survey is live at: **https://synergy-staff-survey.vercel.app/**

## What This Is

A confidential, encrypted survey for your team to give you feedback on their role, wellbeing, tools, and culture. All answers are private and encrypted. You can follow up with each person one-to-one.

## For Your Team

1. **Share this link:** https://synergy-staff-survey.vercel.app/
2. They pick their company (Portal or Synergy)
3. Enter their work email to verify
4. Fill out the survey (~15 minutes)
5. Submit

They'll get a verification code by email — just a 6-digit number to confirm it's really them.

## For You (Nigel & Michael)

1. **Go to:** https://synergy-staff-survey.vercel.app/results
2. **Log in** with your email
3. **Verify** with the code sent to you
4. **See:**
   - Charts (eNPS, workload, support scores)
   - All free-text answers grouped by question
   - Individual responses with staff names
   - Filter by company or person
   - Export to CSV anytime

## That's It

Responses come in encrypted. You can follow up with each person directly. No one else can see the results.
